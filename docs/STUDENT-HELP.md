# Student help: saving your progress

**Your progress saves in this browser only.** No account, no name, nothing sent anywhere. If you use a different computer, browser or device, your progress is not there unless you bring it with a progress code.

## Move your progress to another device

1. Click **My progress** at the top.
2. Click **Get my progress code**. Copy the code, or take a photo of the QR code.
3. **Save your code somewhere safe, like your notes app. Anyone with your code can load your progress.**
4. On the other device, open the app, click **My progress**, type or paste the code into **Enter my progress code**, and click **Load my progress**. (Or scan the QR code with the device's camera.)
5. If that device already has progress, choose **Keep the newer progress for each activity** (usually best) or **Replace everything with the code**.

Typing mistakes are fine to make: the app checks the code and tells you if a character is wrong. It ignores spaces, dashes and lower case.
Your code changes as you work. Get a new one each time you switch devices.

## Stamps and sound

Earn up to three stamps in each activity: one for the game's goal, one for getting every Check it question right on the first try, and one for finishing all four steps. See them in **Stamps**. Stamps are just for you and are never taken away. Turn sound on or off with the button in the top bar.

## Hand in your progress summary

1. Click **My progress**, then **Download summary (image)**.
2. Find the image in your Downloads folder.
3. Upload it to your class page on the school's learning platform, if your teacher asks.

The app never sends your summary. You choose whether to hand it in.

**Careful:** private or incognito windows, and clearing your browsing data, delete saved progress. Get your code first.
